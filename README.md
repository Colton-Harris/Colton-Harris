I build personalized AI and agent-driven products, with a focus on useful model behavior and practical business workflows.

## Gyld — specialized models people can own

[Gyld](https://gyld.dev/) explores niche-specific AI models, custom training, and hosted access. I directed niche selection, base-model choices, the Apple Silicon/MLX approach, and intended model behavior; coding agents executed much of the implementation and evaluation under my direction.

[Ten public model releases on Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/flywheel-ai) include safetensors and GGUF artifacts. **flywheel-ai is Gyld’s legacy model namespace.** Personalized training from permissioned context is a direction I am exploring, not a claim of demonstrated continual-learning gains.
